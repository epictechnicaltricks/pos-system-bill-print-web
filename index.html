Your MPT-II is a classic-Bluetooth (SPP) printer — browsers can't talk to that directly, so "Bluetooth (BLE)" will keep asking to pair and never actually print on it.

Use Print via RawBT app instead: install the free "RawBT Print Service" app from the Play Store, open it once and pair/select your MPT-II inside RawBT's own settings, then just tap this button each time — it hands the bill straight to RawBT, no repeated pairing.
